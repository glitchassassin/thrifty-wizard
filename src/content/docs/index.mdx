Agentic engineering - that's a mouthful for you - is on fire right now. So many voices, tools,
opinions, and nonsense. And everyone wants a piece of that delicious enterprise AI spending. It's
easy to rack up a fortune in expenses before you know it. But some of us have to figure this out on
our own with limited budgets.

That's where I come in. I've been drinking from the fire hose and testing all this stuff. Well,
okay, not _all_ of it. But enough to figure out how to do this on the cheap.

I'm a professional, so I aim to get the best results I can. I'm also lazy, so I don't want to work
harder than I have to. And I don't have a generous corporate budget funding my tokenmaxxing. Time is
money, and money is money, as they say. I aim to make the most of my resources.

If you're coming into this from outside the software industry, lured in by the siren song of
vibe-coding, welcome. There's still a lot of background knowledge about software that will be useful
to you, even if you don't read the code. Check out the [Apprentice's Path](/guides/apprentice/).

If you're an experienced software engineer already, you might want to start with the
[Journeyman's Path](/guides/journeyman/) for background to understand the new technologies that are
transforming the world.

When you're ready, the [Master's Path](/guides/master/) will help you put the pieces together and
design your own ideal toolkit.

Do you have to follow them in order? Nah. Wander around and explore whatever catches your fancy. But
if you don't know where to start, pick a path and take a stroll.
